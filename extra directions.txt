A few questions about HW2 have come up, so here are the answers for the whole class.

1. What kind of hyperparameter tuning is not allowed?
You are free to tune hyperparameters yourself (learning rate, batch size, optimizer, number of layers, augmentation and so on). What is not allowed is using a hyperparameter optimization or neural architecture search tool or library, such as Optuna, Ray Tune, Keras Tuner, or NAS methods like DARTS.

2. Can we combine the 70% and 10% data for cross-validation or a different split (e.g., 80/20)?
Yes. You may combine the training and validation sets and split them however you like, including for cross-validation. Just make sure you do not use any external data.

3. When is the assignment due?
Sunday, November 8, 2026, at 11:59 pm, as shown on Canvas. The instructions will be updated to match.

4. How does the live evaluation work?
- After the deadline, one member of each group books a time with me within two weeks (November 9–22). You can reach me by email or Teams; please include your group number and a few times that work for you.
- The evaluation takes place in person in my office, PGH 370, where I will give you the test set and you can test with your model.
- No slides or statistical analysis are needed. Just be ready to briefly explain your architecture, data augmentation and training choices.

If you have other questions, please let me know.
